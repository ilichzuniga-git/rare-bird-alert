Privacy Policy

Birder's Best Friend · last updated September 24, 2026

Birder's Best Friend is a free, non-commercial Android app that shows rare bird sightings in Los Angeles and Orange Counties. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. This page explains the little data the app does handle.

What the app collects

  • Location, only when you send a report. When you tap "I refound it" or "I dipped" and have allowed location access, your phone's coordinates are sent with the report and stored on our server. Reports work without location if you decline.
  • A push notification token. If you allow notifications, the app registers a token (issued by Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) with our server so it can send rare-bird alerts. The token identifies the app install for delivery, not you.

Reports and tokens are not linked to your name, email, or any account; the app never asks for them.

What stays on your phone

The "Near me" view uses your location only on your device, to sort birds by distance and show your spot on the map. That location is not sent to our server.

How it's used

Only to run the app: report locations help show whether a bird is still being seen, and push tokens are used to deliver alerts. We do not sell, rent, or share this data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Services the app talks to

Like any app that loads content from the internet, your phone connects directly to these services, which see your IP address under their own privacy policies:

  • iNaturalist — bird photos
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles
  • unpkg — the Leaflet map library
  • Expo and Google Firebase — delivering push notifications

Links you tap (eBird pages, directions) open in your browser or maps app and are governed by those services.

Security and retention

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our server briefly keeps IP addresses in memory to limit report spam; they are not stored. Push tokens are deleted when they stop working (for example, after you uninstall the app). Reports are kept to show each bird's history.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location access in Android settings; the app still works.
  • Turn off notifications in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to stop alerts.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
