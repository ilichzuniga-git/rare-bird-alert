Concept 2 — Live Chase

Built for the moment a birder is chasing: dark UI (easy on the eyes at dawn), a live "updated" pulse, and a stat strip answering "what's hot right now". Rarity is color-coded glow on both screens; the freshest exceptional birds get big banner cards, older ones collapse to compact rows. The map is full-bleed with a floating pill nav, your location, pulsing pins for continuing birds, and a swipeable card carousel with one-tap Refound / Dipped.
