Concept 1 — Field Guide

Leans into birding heritage: warm paper tones, a serif species name like a printed guide, and "plates" for each bird. Rarity is typographic (small caps in rust / ochre / moss) instead of pill badges. Status reads as a colored dot + plain sentence. The map uses a muted atlas style with photo medallions ringed by rarity, and a preview card with Refound / Dipped right on the map.
