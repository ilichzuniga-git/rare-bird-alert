Concept 3 — Map + Sheet

One screen instead of two tabs, like Apple / Google Maps: the map is always underneath and the list lives in a draggable bottom sheet. Pull it up for the full list (left) or tap a pin and it snaps to that bird's detail (right). A hero card spotlights the rarest bird; the rest are tidy grouped rows with photo avatars, a status dot, and a soft rarity badge. The sheet also shows a per-day "seen / dipped" strip so you can judge a chase at a glance.
